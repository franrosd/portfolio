Project focus

Explore which customer and shopping-behaviour variables are associated with annual spending, then estimate potential yearly customer value.

Key insights

Average session time, total promotions, bounce rate, tenure, and income were significant predictors in the final stepwise regression model. Estimated annual spending for a new customer was about Rp10.98 million.

Analytical approach

Pearson correlation · Correlation matrix · Stepwise multiple regression

Interpreting the estimate

The R² and adjusted R² describe the model fit in the analysis. The Rp10.98 million figure is an estimated annual spending value, not a guaranteed amount for an individual customer.
